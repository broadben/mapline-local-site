The problem

70% of calls go to the top 3 on Google Maps. If you're ranked 7 or lower, you're invisible.

Someone searching "roofer near me," "dentist near me," or any service you provide sees three businesses in the map pack. The rest scroll past. Being ranked #7 might feel like "on the first page" — it's not. It's the 28th option most searchers never open.

The businesses ranking above you aren't necessarily better than you. They're more visible. Fixing that is a very specific body of work — one most agencies skip in favor of the usual SEO theater.

What we do

Three things that actually move rankings.

01

Google Business Profile optimization

Category tuning, services expansion, photo depth, description rewrites. The single highest-impact action in local SEO — and the one most agencies treat as an afterthought. GBP signals are 32% of local ranking weight.

02

Review velocity + response

Google now weights recent reviews and response rate more than raw count. A business with 80 reviews and steady weekly flow outranks one with 200 stale reviews. We build the system that keeps both high.

03

Citation authority + NAP consistency

Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, industry-specific directories — the authoritative listings Google cross-references. We fix inconsistencies across every platform Google reads.

Methodology

No black box. You see the data.

We track a 49-point geo-grid across your service area every month. You see exactly where you rank for each target keyword — not a vague "you're on page 1 now" report. When we change your primary category or add 20 services, we show you the before/after and the impact on rankings 30 days later.

Most local SEO is optimization theater: reports that celebrate metrics that don't matter. We measure the three things Google actually weighs for local search — primary category accuracy, review velocity, and profile completeness — and keep every other tactic secondary.
